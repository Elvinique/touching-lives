Growth Track is the best way to go from *attending* to *belonging*. Over four Sunday afternoons we share a meal and walk through four simple questions:

1. **Week 1 — Our story.** Where we came from, what we believe, and why we exist.
2. **Week 2 — Your story.** Spiritual growth and the next step that fits you.
3. **Week 3 — Your place.** How God wired you, and where you can serve.
4. **Week 4 — Your part.** Life groups, generosity, and going public with faith.

Lunch is provided each week, kids' ministry is available, and there's zero pressure to sign up for anything you're not ready for.

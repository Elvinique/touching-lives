Some things are too heavy for the car ride home. That's what this night is for.

We fill the auditorium with candlelight and quiet music, then invite you to bring whatever you're carrying — a diagnosis, a prodigal, a job search, a broken relationship — and leave it at the feet of the One who cares.

**The evening includes:**

- Guided prayer stations around the room
- A team of trained prayer partners available all night
- Communion for those who want it
- Time to simply sit in stillness if that's what you need

Come alone or bring a friend. You don't have to pray out loud. You don't have to stay the whole time. You just have to come.

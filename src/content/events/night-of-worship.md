Some nights are meant to slow down. The Night of Worship is one of them — a candlelit evening of songs, Scripture, and unhurried space to simply be with God.

**The evening flows like this:**

- A full night of worship led by our band, with moments of prayer woven through
- Space for quiet reflection and responding in your own way
- Prayer teams available at the front throughout the night
- Communion to close

Come early, bring someone who needs a quiet night, and stay as long as you like. Kids' ministry is available for ages 0–5.

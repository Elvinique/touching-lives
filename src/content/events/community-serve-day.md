Community Serve Day is the church leaving the building. Every year we pause our normal Sunday rhythm and spend a morning loving our city the way Jesus did — with our hands.

**Serve projects include:**

- School supply packing for local teachers
- Neighborhood & park cleanups
- Serving meals with our city partners
- Visiting and encouraging our senior friends
- Sorting donations for partner ministries

We'll kick off with a short rally, coffee, and assignment to your team — then scatter and serve. Kids and teens serve alongside their families on family-friendly projects.

**No sign-up experience required. Just show up with open hands.**

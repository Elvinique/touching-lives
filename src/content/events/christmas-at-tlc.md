Christmas at Touching Lives is our favorite week of the year. The whole building glows, the band pulls out all the stops, and we tell the story of God coming near in a way the whole family can enter.

**This year's service:**

- A candlelit night of carols old and new
- The Christmas story told through music and word
- A brief, hopeful message about what it means that God is with us
- Kids' programming during all three services

Three identical services on Sunday, December 20: **9:00 AM, 11:00 AM, and 5:00 PM.** Services last 60 minutes.

**Bring a friend, a neighbor, or someone you've been meaning to invite for years. There's a seat for them.**

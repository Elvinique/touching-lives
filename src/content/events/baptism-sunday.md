Baptism Sunday is one of our loudest, most joyful Sundays of the year. It's the day we get to watch people publicly say, "Jesus, my life is yours."

**If you're considering baptism:**

- It's for anyone who has decided to follow Jesus — no matter how long ago
- Our team will walk with you through a short conversation and preparation
- You can be baptized at any service; we'll help you pick the moment

Even if you're not being baptized, come. There's nothing like watching someone step into the water. It's the kind of morning that reminds you why the church exists.

**Ready to take the step? Talk to anyone on our team after a service, or reach out through the contact page.**

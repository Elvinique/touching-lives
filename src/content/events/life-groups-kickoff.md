Sunday is a great start, but life happens Monday through Saturday. Life groups are how we do life together — real people, real kitchens, real conversations.

**At the Kickoff you can:**

- Meet group leaders from across the city in person
- Browse groups by neighborhood, season of life, and interest
- Sign up on the spot — no commitment games
- Grab coffee and a pastry while you look

Groups meet weekly or biweekly, most in homes, and there's genuinely a group for every stage: young adults, families with kids, empty nesters, men's and women's groups, and more.

**Not sure what to look for? Start with the table that has the most people laughing.**

Every student wonders if they're enough — enough for their family, their friends, their future. This weekend is our answer: you are more than enough, because the One who made you says so.

**The weekend includes:**

- Three main sessions with worship, teaching, and response time
- Breakout tracks on identity, friendship, and faith that lasts
- Games, a late-night hang, and more snacks than we'll admit to
- Small groups with leaders who actually care

Students stay with host homes and leaders overnight; drop-off and pick-up details are shared after registration.

**Early-bird pricing ends October 1. Scholarships are always available — just ask.**

We were never meant to do life alone. Life groups are our answer to that — small circles of people who meet in homes across the city to eat, study Scripture, pray, and simply do life together.

## Find your fit

With 60+ groups, there's one for you:

- **By neighborhood** — from North Austin to Buda
- **By season** — young families, singles, empty nesters, new believers
- **By interest** — men's, women's, couples', hiking, cooking, you name it
- **Online groups** — for those who travel or live far away

## What a group looks like

Groups meet weekly or biweekly for about 90 minutes: a meal or snacks, a conversation around the weekend's message or a book of the Bible, and time to pray for one another. Most groups run in semesters with a summer break.

**Not sure where to start?** Come to the Life Groups Kickoff in January, or email us and we'll help you find your people.

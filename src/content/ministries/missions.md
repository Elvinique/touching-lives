"Sent" is our name for everything we do beyond our own walls — because we believe every follower of Jesus is sent somewhere.

## Our city

- **Community Serve Day** — a citywide morning of serving every fall
- **Partner ministries** — food pantry, tutoring, and family support partners we serve monthly
- **Neighborhood tables** — dinners that bring together neighbors, block by block

## The nations

We partner with churches and ministries in **12 countries**, supporting national leaders, sponsoring community projects, and sending short-term teams.

## Your part

You don't have to fly anywhere to be a missionary. You can pack supplies, host a team, pray monthly for our partners, or give toward a specific project.

**Come to a monthly send meeting (1st Tuesday) and hear what God is doing — you might be surprised what He invites you into.**

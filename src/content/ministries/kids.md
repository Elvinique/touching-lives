Kids don't just learn about God at Touching Lives Kids — they meet Him through people who show up week after week.

## What to expect

Every child is checked in securely at our welcome stations and picked up with a matching tag. Our classrooms are staffed by trained, background-checked volunteers, and each room follows a rhythm designed for its age group:

- **Nursery (0–2):** a warm, gentle space with caregivers who pray over the littlest ones
- **Preschool (3–5):** songs, simple Bible stories, and play that teaches God's love
- **Elementary (K–5th):** high-energy worship, small groups, and a Bible story they'll actually remember

## Growing faith at home

Parents are the most important faith-shapers in a child's life. Each week we send home a simple "family conversation" card — one question and one activity to keep the story going around your dinner table.

We'd love to meet your family. Kids check in 15 minutes before each service at the Kids Welcome desk.

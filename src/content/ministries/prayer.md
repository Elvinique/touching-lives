Before anything else happens at Touching Lives, we pray. The Quiet Place is the ministry that undergirds everything — a team of trained intercessors and a prayer room that's never locked for long.

## Ways to join

- **Prayer partners** — trained volunteers who pray with people after services and at special events
- **Intercessors** — weekly teams praying through the church's prayer wall and requests
- **Prayer room** — open during services and Tuesday evenings; anyone is welcome to sit, pray, or just be still
- **Prayer groups** — small groups whose main agenda is praying together

## How we handle requests

Every request submitted through the website is confidential, prayed over by name, and — when the submitter asks — followed up with by a team member.

**If you'd like to learn to pray more confidently, ask about our prayer mentoring — a few sessions with a seasoned intercessor who'll walk with you.**

Every Sunday, hundreds of people encounter God through the work of a team of volunteers: the band, the vocalists, the sound and lights crew, the camera operators, the people who paint and write and design.

## Where you can serve

- **Music** — band, vocalists, and choir (we rehearse Wednesday evenings)
- **Tech** — sound, lights, and live-stream production (training provided)
- **Creative** — video, photography, graphic design, lobby art
- **Story** — writing, editing, and behind-the-scenes storytelling

## More than a stage

Our heart isn't a polished production — it's helping people meet Jesus. We rehearse hard and pray harder, and we'd rather have a raw moment of God's presence than a perfect performance.

**Auditions and interviews happen a few times a year, and no one is asked to serve alone.** Reach out and we'll find your spot.

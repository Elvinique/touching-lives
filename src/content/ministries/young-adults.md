College, first jobs, new cities, big questions. Young adulthood is an exciting and lonely season, and The Common exists so no one walks it alone.

## What we do

- **Gatherings** — worship, teaching, and real conversation on 2nd and 4th Sundays
- **Life groups** — neighborhood-based groups for young adults across the city
- **Hangouts** — hikes, game nights, and the occasional very serious taco crawl
- **Serve together** — young adults leading our city projects and kids' ministry

## Where you fit

Whether you're a student at UT, working your first real job, or figuring out the next chapter, there's a seat (and usually a good coffee) waiting for you.

The first step is the easiest: show up on a 2nd or 4th Sunday and look for the people laughing the loudest.

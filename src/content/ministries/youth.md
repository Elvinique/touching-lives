Middle and high school are a lot. The Gathering is a place to breathe — a weekly gathering where students can be real about their questions and find faith that survives the school week.

## Wednesday nights

Every Wednesday at 7:00 PM we pack the student center for games, worship, a message that speaks their language, and small groups where students are actually known.

Beyond Wednesday, The Gathering runs:

- **Summer camps & conferences** — including our More Than Enough weekend
- **Serve Saturdays** — students leading the way in loving our city
- **Sunday gathering** — a student-only hang before the 11:00 service

## Leaders who stay

Our volunteer leaders are background-checked, trained, and in it for the long haul. They show up to ball games, remember birthdays, and pray for students by name all week long.

New student? Just show up. The first Wednesday is the easiest one — we promise.

The disciples had just returned from their first mission trip — buzzing, exhausted, full of stories. And Jesus' response wasn't "well done, now here's the next assignment." It was: *"Come with me by yourselves to a quiet place and get some rest."*

In a culture that worships productivity, that sentence is revolutionary.

## Rest is a command, not a reward

Notice the sequence. Rest comes first — before the crowds, before the next big thing. Jesus doesn't offer rest as a prize for finishing. He commands it as the posture of a trusting life.

If we only rest after everything is done, we never rest.

## The world runs on hurry

Our devices are engineered to keep us from stillness. The notifications, the comparison, the endless feed — it's all designed to make us feel behind.

Rest isn't just a break from that. It's a statement that our worth isn't measured by our output. That's why rest is resistance.

## A quiet place, with Him

Jesus didn't say "go rest alone." He said *come with me*. The goal isn't just rest — it's rest with the one who carried the world and still chose to sleep in the boat.

This week, practice one deliberate pause. Turn off the notifications. Breathe. Remember you are loved before you are useful.

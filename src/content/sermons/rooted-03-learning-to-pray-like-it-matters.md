The disciples watched Jesus pray and they didn't ask for more power or better teaching. They asked, "Lord, teach us to pray." They had seen something in His praying they wanted for themselves.

## Prayer is not performance

Jesus opens with two words that quietly dismantle religious performance: *Our Father*. Not a distant judge, not a vending machine — a Father.

When prayer feels like trying to impress someone, we've missed it. Prayer is conversation between children and a good Father.

## Ask, seek, knock

Jesus is shameless about persistence. Ask. Seek. Knock. Not because God is reluctant, but because the asking is changing us.

The friend in the parable got what he needed not because he deserved it, but because he wouldn't stop asking. Prayer is how we learn what we actually need.

## The Spirit, the better gift

Jesus ends with a promise that reorients everything: if earthly parents know how to give good gifts, how much more will the Father give the Holy Spirit to those who ask.

We don't always get what we want. But we always get Him.

Pray like it matters — because it does.
